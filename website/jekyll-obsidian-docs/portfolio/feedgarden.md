Feedgarden collects news and discussions into a feed with English and Chinese summaries. Its archive layout supports both a quick scan of the day's entries and a longer reading session.

- Filter by source or topic, browse by date and page through older entries.
- Save entries to Favorites and switch to Read in full to read complete summaries within the list.
- Export selected entries as Markdown for reading or keeping elsewhere.
