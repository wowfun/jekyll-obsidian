Sinputer's Site brings technical notes, essays and poetry together. It uses Jekyll Obsidian's Minimal theme to give each kind of writing a place while keeping recent work easy to find.

- The homepage pairs a short introduction with recent posts.
- The blog groups articles by year, with a timeline for browsing older writing.
- Custom sections collect projects, passages from the Tao Te Ching and favorite resources alongside the blog.
